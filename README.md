# 💡 IdeaVault – Startup Idea Sharing Platform

**Live Site:** [https://ideavault.vercel.app](https://ideavault.vercel.app)

## About
IdeaVault is a web-based platform where users can share innovative startup ideas, explore ideas posted by others, and engage through comments and discussions.

## Features

 **Secure Authentication** – Email/password and Google login with JWT, powered by BetterAuth
 **Idea Management** – Submit, edit, and delete your startup ideas with full CRUD functionality
 **Search & Filter** – Search ideas by title and filter by category or date range
 **Comment System** – Add, edit, and delete comments on any idea
 **Dark / Light Theme** – Toggle between dark and light mode globally from the navbar
 **Trending Ideas** – Discover the most viewed ideas from the community
 **Top Contributors** – See the most active members of the platform
 **Fully Responsive** – Works on mobile, tablet, and desktop

## Tech Stack

**Frontend:** Next.js, Tailwind CSS, BetterAuth, Axios  
**Backend:** Express.js, MongoDB, Mongoose, JWT
